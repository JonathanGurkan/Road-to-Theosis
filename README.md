### What is Theosis?
Theosis (Ancient Greek: θε⍵σις) is the process of becoming like God. It is the goal of the Christian life. We as Christians are called to be the salt and light of the world and to give flavour and light to the world. 

### What is Road to Theosis?
Road to Theosis is a companion app to help you on your journey to become like Christ. It is a tool to help you track your prayer life, sin purificaction progress, bible reading and more! These are all customisable to make sure every user can get the most out of it while not being distracted by the app itself. The app visually mimics a normal Apple-style app so you feel familiar with it's navigation and it's easy to use first hand. 

### What does it do?
The app consists of a dashboard and a timeline. The dashboard is where you can see all your stats and get a quick overview of your progress (this, again, is to be taken lightly and one should always be critical of their own progress). It does this with widgets that vary in size and are customizable by the user. 
You can add loggings to the timeline in the form of:
- Stumbles: when you stumble over a sin. This will then have impact on you purity bar per sin so you can see what you stumble with the most so you can dedicate more time and prayer into it.
- Prayers: when you pray you can set your phone in prayer mode to pray without distractions. While in prayer mode you can choose to have it count your prayer time in the background. After prayer you ca write down what you felt, prayer for, and other things into a note field.
- Notes: Just simple things you want to jot down for later.
- Purity changes: The purity bars are able to transition between bars and sliders where with sliders you can change the value of the bar yourself.
